About Harmony Homes

Harmony Homes Independent Living LLC was created to provide more than housing — it was created to provide hope, stability, encouragement, and a true sense of home.

Our homes are designed to offer a peaceful community atmosphere where residents are treated with dignity and respect while being encouraged toward independence, accountability, and personal growth.

Our Mission

To provide clean, safe, and welcoming housing for veterans, adults with light disabilities, and individuals seeking stable living while encouraging independence, accountability, and personal growth.

Our Vision

To create safe and supportive homes where individuals can experience stability, encouragement, and the opportunity to build a brighter future.

Meet Mia Jackson

Mia Jackson is a dedicated provider with more than 20 years of experience supporting individuals in home-style living environments. Known for her compassion, consistency, and commitment to helping others grow, she created Harmony Homes to provide a safe place where residents can rebuild confidence, gain stability, and move toward independence.

Her approach combines guidance, encouragement, accountability, and care — creating a welcoming environment where residents are treated with dignity and respect.
