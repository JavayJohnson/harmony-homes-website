FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Harmony Homes Independent Living, LLC.

Are rooms furnished?

Yes. Rooms are fully furnished and designed to provide a comfortable living environment.

How many people share a room?

Rooms are shared with two residents per room.

Who is a good fit for Harmony Homes?

Veterans, SSI/SSDI recipients, reentry individuals, adults with light disabilities, and adults seeking stable housing may be a good fit.

Are the homes drug and alcohol free?

Yes. Harmony Homes maintains drug and alcohol-free homes to support safety and stability.

Is cable TV and internet included?

Yes. Cable TV and internet access are included as part of the home amenities.

Are there house rules?

Yes. Residents are expected to follow respectful community standards, maintain cleanliness, and support a peaceful home environment.

Are cameras used in the home?

Cameras may be installed in common areas for safety and security. Cameras are not placed in private resident areas.

How do I apply?

Visit the Apply Now page and complete the preliminary residency application.

Is availability guaranteed?

No. Housing availability is subject to approval and room availability.
